About this forum

A reading surface over a conversation that already exists.

Drayker’s discussion has always been public, in the issues of its repositories. That is the right place for it — a thread beside the code and documents it concerns survives, and can be argued with by whoever maintains them. It is also a place most people never look, and never think to answer in.

So this site reads all of it and presents it as what it is: conversation. It writes nothing, stores nothing and asks for nothing. Every reply, proposal and correction is published from your own GitHub account, in the repository where it belongs — which means this forum can disappear tomorrow without a single word of the discussion being lost.

A thread is not chatter. It is the first step of the only process there is.

Nothing here is decided by a meeting, a vote in private or a person with a title. A subject is said in the open, routed to whatever owns it, and if it becomes work it ends as a change anyone can read.

01 · SAID IN THE OPEN
Anyone can open a thread without an application or waiting list. Drayker’s membership rules are not operational yet, and no private channel replaces the public record.
02 · ROUTED TO ITS OWNER
If the subject belongs to a component, it moves to that component’s repository, beside the code and the documents it is about.
03 · CUT SMALL ENOUGH
A thread that turns into work is cut into pieces one person can finish. Those carry the label open-function and appear on the board.
04 · ARGUED AGAINST A CHANGE
Whoever claims it opens a pull request to master. From there the discussion is about the actual difference being proposed, not about the idea of it.
05 · MERGED, AND KEPT
The merge is the decision. The thread stays where it is as the record of why it was taken — which is why none of this is deleted.

What this site does not have.

Every absence below is deliberate. A forum that keeps its own accounts, its own copies and its own moderation becomes a second source of truth — and then the argument is about the forum instead of about the work.

NO ACCOUNTS
Your GitHub account is the only identity involved, and it never touches this site.
NO DATABASE
Static pages on GitHub Pages, reading the public API. Nothing is transmitted from here.
NO RANKING
Threads are ordered by last activity unless you choose another order. Nothing is promoted, scored or hidden.
NO MODERATION
Whatever moderation exists is GitHub’s, in the repositories, under the code of conduct published in the draykerdk GitHub organization.
NO STATUS
Labels shown here are the repositories’ own. This site never invents a stage, a phase or a badge.
